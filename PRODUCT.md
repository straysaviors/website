# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS, one page, hosted on GitHub Pages at www.straysaviors.com (CNAME). No build step.

## Users
Donors in India and abroad who land on the site (often from Instagram) and decide in a minute or two whether to give.

## Product Purpose
Stray Saviors rescues, feeds, treats and rehomes stray dogs in India. The site exists to turn visitors into donors.

## Positioning
A small, hands-on volunteer rescue (5 volunteers): donors see the actual dogs they help, not a large institution.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Donation via UPI QR (`assets/upi_qr.jpeg`) and PayPal (QR `assets/paypal_qr.jpg`, link https://www.paypal.me/straysaviors). Both shown with equal weight.
- Instagram: https://www.instagram.com/stray_savior/
- No backend, no forms.

## Brand Commitments
Name is "Stray Saviors" only.

## Evidence on Hand
- Impact numbers (confirmed): 60+ rescued and rehomed, 100+ given medical care, 45+ found loving homes, 5 volunteers.
- Real photos of rescued dogs in `assets/` (feeding, vet care, puppies in a box, portraits).
- No testimonials, no adoption listings, no registration/tax info. Do not invent these.

## Product Principles
1. Real dogs, real photos, before any claim.
2. Donating takes one scan or one tap; never make a donor hunt.
3. Plain, honest copy; small-team voice.
